ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI. It can answer questions, help with coding, writing, studying, brainstorming ideas, and much more.

Students use ChatGPT to understand difficult topics, programmers use it to write and debug code, and creators use it to generate content. When used wisely, it can save time and improve productivity.

Even though ChatGPT is powerful, it is always a good idea to verify important information from reliable sources, especially for studies, health, or legal topics.
