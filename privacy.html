Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to TechWithAmit. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how that information may be used.

Important: This policy should accurately reflect the tools and services actually used on the website. Update it if you add analytics, advertising, cookies or a working contact service.

1. Information We Collect

TechWithAmit does not currently ask visitors to create an account to read its articles. Depending on the hosting provider and any third-party services enabled, technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address or server logs may be processed.

If you contact us through an external email service, the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message, may be processed by that service and your email provider.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Maintain and operate the website.
  • Identify and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Respond to messages or feedback that you send.
  • Protect the website against abuse or suspicious activity.
  • Understand website usage if analytics tools are added.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies through its hosting provider or third-party services. The current website's dark-mode feature uses a page setting and does not itself require a cookie to toggle the theme.

If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to third-party websites, tools or services. These services operate independently and may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their privacy practices.

Please review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing it with personal information.

5. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our content may be useful to students and general audiences. Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through public or third-party services. If you believe personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding access to, correction of or deletion of personal information. To make a request, contact us through the contact details or contact method provided on this website.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.
