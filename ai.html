What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) ek aisi technology hai jo computers ko insaan ki tarah sochne, samajhne aur decision lene me madad karti hai. Aaj AI ka use mobile phones, robots, cars, hospitals, education aur business me ho raha hai.

AI ka use kaha hota hai?

  • Healthcare: disease detect karna aur diagnosis me help
  • Education: personalized learning aur smart tutoring
  • Business: automation, customer support aur data analysis
  • Transport: self-driving cars aur smart navigation

AI kaise kaam karta hai?

AI data ko collect karke patterns ko samajhta hai. Phir us data ke basis par predictions, recommendations aur automation perform karta hai. Machine Learning aur Deep Learning is process ka important part hai.

Final Thought

AI sirf ek technology nahi, balki ek naya digital revolution hai. Agar sahi tarike se use kiya jaye, toh yeh rozmarra ke kaam ko aur behtar, fast aur smart bana sakta hai.
